LLMeter Live Cost — Settings

Everything here is stored locally in your browser. Admin keys are only ever sent to the provider that issued them.

Monthly budget

Currency is USD (what both provider APIs report). When the projected month exceeds this budget the toolbar badge turns red (Pro).

License

Free: 1 provider, 30 days of history. Pro (lifetime): both providers, 90 days, CSV export, projection & budget alert. Get a Pro license

Anthropic amount units

Anthropic's docs disagree on whether cost_report amounts are cents or dollars. Compare the popup with your console: if the extension shows 100× less, switch to dollars.

Provider keys

Keys are masked; remove one to replace it from the popup.

Data
